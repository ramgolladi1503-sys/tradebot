## Grill Me Review

The campaign does not promise that an edge exists. It explicitly accepts:

- `NO_EDGE_FOUND_WITHIN_PREREGISTERED_SEARCH_BUDGET`
- a blocked verdict when fresh confirmation data does not exist
- a terminal negative verdict when the single global holdout fails

It forbids using a failed holdout to tune or select another candidate.

## Hermes Review

Candidate identity is bound by `candidate_bundle_hash` across every stage. Every evidence JSON requires a byte-level SHA-256 sidecar. Missing, malformed, mismatched, contaminated, fallback-derived, or out-of-order evidence fails closed.

The campaign contract caps the complete search at 40 preregistered variants.
