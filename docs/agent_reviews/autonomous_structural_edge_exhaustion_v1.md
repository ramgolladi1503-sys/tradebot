## Acceptance Proof

The dedicated GitHub Actions workflow must:

1. hydrate only the pinned constituent/index LFS object;
2. pass the autonomous causality/sealed-tail tests plus reused Pattern Atlas certification tests;
3. execute the two-batch 12-family campaign physically;
4. verify all 12 families were attempted, failed families were not reopened, global q-threshold is 0.05, and the unopened tail remains sealed unless robustness survivors exist;
5. upload the complete evidence artifact.

A negative result is an accepted research result. No threshold may be relaxed to force a survivor.
