# Agent Review Evidence — Autonomous Structural Edge Exhaustion V1

## Agent Work Contract

Research-only autonomous structural-edge discovery and certification layered on the existing Pattern Atlas evidence framework. Search predeclared orthogonal constituent/index information families under one shared chronological split and one campaign-wide multiple-testing budget. Failed families must not be reopened or threshold-tuned.

The current campaign has three batches and 18 distinct information families. Batch C was selected from information primitives absent from Batches A/B, not from favorable outcomes observed in prior batches. Expanding the campaign tightens the global BH q threshold to 2.5% rather than resetting the statistical slate.

## Scope Guard

In scope: source authority, observation-only constituent-universe selection, outcome-blind state-family discovery, recurring transition freezing, hypothesis freezing, development outcome attachment, campaign-wide statistical screen, chronological validation/WFA, robustness attacks, one-shot unopened-tail testing, exhaustion ledger, and research strategy specifications for statistical survivors only.

Out of scope: broker calls, order placement/modification/cancellation, live/paper activation, risk/ranking changes, production strategy registry changes, option translation, POST_CAS pooling, retroactive threshold tuning, reopening failed families, and using Batch A/B near misses to define Batch C.

## What This PR Does Not Prove

This PR does not prove a full structural edge merely because a statistical candidate survives. Point-in-time NIFTY constituent membership is not available in the proxy corpus and remains an explicit survivorship/membership authority limitation. It does not prove an options edge, execution edge, POST_CAS robustness, live readiness, paper readiness, broker safety beyond non-use, or profitability outside the certified historical evidence.

A terminal negative result from this PR means the predeclared information families available from this pinned constituent/index OHLCV proxy corpus were exhausted under the frozen campaign budget. It does not mean all possible market strategies or future richer-data mechanisms are impossible.
