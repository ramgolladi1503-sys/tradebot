# TEP v1 — Vision

Status: DRAFT  
Version: 1.0.0-draft

## Vision statement

TEP enables governed autonomous engineering and research workflows to execute from durable mission contracts without requiring a human to shuttle prompts, logs and decisions between tools.

Autonomy is subordinate to evidence and authority.

## Problems TEP solves

1. Human-as-router workflows between architecture agents, execution agents, CI and GitHub.
2. Loss of state/context when a worker or process stops.
3. Repeated rediscovery of repository, research and operational facts.
4. One-shot agents that stop at repairable blockers.
5. Authority ambiguity around GitHub, cleanup and live-market actions.
6. PR/worktree proliferation caused by local optimization rather than integration policy.
7. Evidence claims that outgrow what the underlying session/test actually proved.
8. Research loops vulnerable to leakage, selection pressure and strategy loyalty.

## Desired operating model

A product owner defines/approves goals and governance. Architecture is frozen in specifications. Missions encode executable objectives. TBOS schedules durable tasks. Services own domain semantics. Workers execute bounded tasks. Drivers adapt external systems. Validators determine contract satisfaction. Evidence and knowledge retain provenance. Humans receive only genuine decisions/escalations.

## Success characteristics

TEP v1 is successful when it can demonstrably:

- resume a mission after process failure without losing committed state;
- continue independent work while another task waits;
- classify and route repairable blockers rather than stopping generically;
- enforce capability-specific authority at mutation boundaries;
- integrate repository work serially against refreshed source authority;
- preserve unique evidence before cleanup;
- keep live/trading authority false unless explicitly granted;
- bind important claims to reproducible evidence;
- execute at least repository-consolidation and read-only observation missions without a human acting as message router.

## Non-goals

TEP is not an AGI controller, trading strategy, profit engine, replacement for independent validation, or blanket automation framework for every personal task.

## Product principle

The platform should reduce coordination complexity, not merely move it into a larger codebase. A capability that cannot justify its ownership, evidence, authority and maintenance cost should not be added.
