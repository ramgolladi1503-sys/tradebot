# TEP v1 — Guiding Principles

Status: DRAFT  
Version: 1.0.0-draft

1. **Architecture before implementation.** Freeze contracts before workers write production code.
2. **Evidence before conclusions.** Claims are bounded by exact evidence.
3. **Authority before mutation.** Access is not authorization.
4. **Preservation before deletion.** Unknown provenance means preserve.
5. **Current authority before integration.** Recheck source/dependencies immediately before irreversible integration.
6. **Declarative missions over prompt chains.** Durable goals/state do not live in chat.
7. **Services own semantics.** Drivers adapt; workers execute; schedulers schedule.
8. **Workers are replaceable.** No platform truth depends on one model/vendor.
9. **Failure is typed state.** Repairable, waiting, human-only, live-gated and invalidated are different outcomes.
10. **Independent work continues.** Waiting does not freeze the fleet.
11. **Idempotency at mutation boundaries.** Crash/retry must not duplicate irreversible work.
12. **Observability is part of correctness.** Operators must know what ran, why, under what authority and with what evidence.
13. **Complexity has a budget.** New modules/dependencies require justification.
14. **No strategy loyalty.** Sunk engineering cost is not research evidence.
15. **Selection pressure is tracked.** Autonomous discovery strengthens validation requirements as search breadth grows.
16. **Operational correctness is separate from economic validity.** Stable runtime does not prove edge.
17. **Live evidence is session/SHA specific.** Historical or adjacent live success is not fresh proof.
18. **CI convergence does not mean CI weakening.** Repair causes, not gates.
19. **Successor branches are exceptional.** Prefer safe repair/update of existing integration surfaces.
20. **Humans decide; humans do not route.** Escalate genuine decisions, not routine tool output.
