# Leading-Wing Pressure Persistence V1

Research-only discovery of a higher-occurrence NIFTY buy-option continuation mechanism.

The frozen hypothesis is that premium expansion persists when one option wing leads while the same-strike opposite wing decays and option-surface breadth, OI, volume, acceptance or directional strike-mass migration confirms the pressure.

Governance:

- ten preregistered mechanism variants;
- four expanding chronological WFA folds with training-only quantiles;
- at least 100 OOF trades across 70 sessions;
- state-onset signals, maximum two per session, twenty-minute cooldown;
- exact next-minute same-contract open entry and exact five-minute outcome;
- 0.1% base friction and 1.0% stress friction;
- latest 25% holdout opened only for at most two frozen OOF survivors;
- mirror-side and five-minute delayed-entry holdout controls;
- no post-outcome threshold rescue;
- no broker calls, paper trading, live trading or production registration.
