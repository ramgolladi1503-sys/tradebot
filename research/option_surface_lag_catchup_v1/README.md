# Option-Surface Lag Catch-Up V1

Research-only discovery of a cross-sectional NIFTY buy-option mechanism.

Hypothesis: when an expiry/option wing is rising broadly but one liquid contract lags that confirmed surface, the lagging contract may catch up over the next five minutes. This is distinct from outright momentum and capitulation reversal.

Governance:

- ten preregistered variants;
- four expanding chronological WFA folds with training-only quantiles;
- at least 100 OOF trades across 70 sessions;
- maximum two state-onset signals per session with a twenty-minute cooldown;
- exact next-minute same-contract open entry and exact five-minute outcome;
- 0.1% base and 1.0% stress friction;
- latest 25% holdout opened only for at most two frozen OOF survivors;
- same-strike mirror, five-minute delay and same-surface leader controls;
- no post-outcome rescue or threshold change;
- no broker, paper, live or production action.
