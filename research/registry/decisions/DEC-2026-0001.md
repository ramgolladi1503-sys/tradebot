## Context

## Decision

Adopt the manual's architecture, milestone portfolio, work-package portfolio, sprint execution standard, authority semantics, QA model, calibration rules, and runtime boundary as the MROS program baseline.

The repository roadmap is updated to match the manual. Any future change to milestone intent, work-package intent, authority grades, or gate semantics requires a new recorded decision and impact review.

## Impact review

### Prior work

Existing Sprint-001 governance artifacts are retained as reusable bootstrap evidence. They are **not** automatically relabeled as completion of S001-S030 or acceptance of WP001-WP006. Each controlled sprint/WP must still satisfy its acceptance criteria, evidence manifest, independent review/attack requirements, and Definition of Done.

### Premature M2 work

PR #812 and its calibration artifacts are retained as provisional evidence and reusable implementation material. M2 progression is paused because the adopted dependency order places WP001-WP006 / S001-S030 before WP007 / S031. No calibration evidence is deleted or promoted by this decision.

### CI

Green CI remains necessary but is not sufficient for research acceptance. Scientific, data, statistical, and authority contracts must also be evidenced.

## Non-negotiable invariants

- Repository-backed evidence outranks model confidence or human memory.
- No discovering agent grants final authority to its own result.
- Calibration precedes strong negative-certifier interpretation.
- Outcome-blind hypothesis formulation precedes outcome attachment.
- Multiple-testing budgets are not reset because a campaign failed.
- Sealed holdouts are opened only under predeclared rules.
- Runtime consumes certified knowledge and does not conduct research.
- Unknown and Rejected are valid outcomes.

## Rollback

This decision may only be superseded by a later DEC record with explicit evidence, alternatives, impact analysis, migration consequences, and review trigger. Historical evidence and this decision must remain queryable.
