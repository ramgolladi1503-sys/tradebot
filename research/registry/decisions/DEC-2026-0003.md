# DEC-2026-0003 — Adopt Multi-Reviewer + Multi-Auditor Governance

Status: ACTIVE
Decision date: 2026-08-08
Decision class: PROGRAM_GOVERNANCE_EXTENSION
Authority: User-approved MROS Master Autonomous Execution Directive v2.0.
Operational authority: NONE

## Context

MROS already required independent attack and had bootstrap Review Board infrastructure. The v2.0 directive strengthens the institutional gate by separating implementation review from evidence/process audit and requiring at least ten valid independent artifacts in each layer before ordinary sprint acceptance after bootstrap authorization.

## Decision

For normal automated authority no earlier than S003, use the sequence:

`native validation -> exact-head freeze -> >=10 independent reviewers -> severity-aware review aggregation -> >=10 independent auditors -> severity-aware audit aggregation -> sprint acceptance`.

Reviewers attack implementation correctness. Auditors attack the defensibility of the acceptance claim and evidence chain. Review and audit are distinct roles and neither may be collapsed into simple majority voting.

Any confirmed CRITICAL or unresolved MAJOR blocks. Mandatory UNKNOWN blocks. Dissent is preserved. A repaired head invalidates review/audit authority from earlier heads and requires new native validation plus new review and audit rounds.

The default maximum is five full repair/review/audit rounds per sprint before architecture escalation.

## Bootstrap Boundary

The automated Review/Audit Board may not certify S002 or its own creation. S002 must close under the pre-existing bootstrap-independent review mechanism. The combined board must then pass deterministic calibration and a genuinely independent bootstrap review before a controlled decision may mark it authorized for normal use.

## Calibration

Calibration must include known-bad defects, known-good controls, stale-head evidence, fake independence, MAJOR-hidden-by-majority, mandatory UNKNOWN, malformed schema, aggregation correctness, adjudication correctness, exact-head invalidation, M9 hard stop, and runtime-authority boundary.

Until executed calibration and independent bootstrap review pass, board status remains `IMPLEMENTED_NOT_CALIBRATED`, authority `Research / R`.

## Program Boundary

This decision does not accept S002, activate S003, begin M2, begin M9, modify TradeBot runtime, or grant runtime authority. M9 remains `NOT_STARTED`; runtime authority remains `NONE`.

## Observed Facts

The existing v1 board policy allowed an eight-review quorum and had no independent audit layer. The v2 directive requires at least ten independent reviewers plus at least ten independent auditors and a separate audit aggregation gate.

## Inferences

Separating review from audit raises the burden for false acceptance and makes evidence/process defects independently attackable rather than relying solely on implementation reviewers.

## Hypotheses

The combined Review/Audit Board can reduce manual orchestration without increasing false acceptance, provided its calibration and independence gates pass.

## Assumptions

Fresh isolated reviewer/auditor contexts can be provisioned by the execution environment used for ordinary automated operation.

## Destroyers / Falsifiers

- calibration admits a known-bad case;
- calibration rejects a required known-good case;
- non-independent artifacts count toward quorum;
- CRITICAL or MAJOR findings can be majority-voted away;
- stale-head evidence can authorize a repaired head;
- the board self-certifies or retroactively certifies S002;
- runtime authority changes before M9.

## Unknowns

The current repository does not yet contain executed calibration evidence or independent bootstrap acceptance of the combined Review/Audit Board.

## Next Experiment

Complete deterministic calibration of the combined board, then obtain a genuinely independent bootstrap review after S002 is separately accepted.

## Authority Grade

Research / R — governance process authority only; no market or runtime authority.
