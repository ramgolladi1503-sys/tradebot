# Independent Attack — PR #806 Certifier Calibration

## Attack ID

`ATTACK-CAL-2026-0001`

## Target

`CAL-2026-0001` — frozen PR #806 Stage-6 certifier and the provisional reverse-certification claim that it is functional but underpowered for sparse modest effects.

## Attack Objective

Attempt to destroy or narrow calibration authority without rescuing any market hypothesis.

## Attack 1 — Always-Fail Implementation

Destroyer: known positive effects should still be rejected at large effect sizes.

Result: destroyed. Independent dense recovery rises from 0% at +2 bps to 31.94% at +5, 81.02% at +8, and 99.38% at +15. The implementation is not an unconditional always-fail screen.

## Attack 2 — Hidden False-Positive Explosion

Destroyer: null worlds should frequently produce full Stage-6 survivors.

Result: not observed under the tested null. Independent reproduction found one full Stage-6 false positive across 1,000 global session-sign-flip worlds. However, this does not prove control under arbitrary dependence or misspecification.

## Attack 3 — Adequate Power for Modest Sparse Effects

Destroyer of the provisional underpower claim: sparse +5 bps effects should be recovered reliably.

Result: attack failed; underpower claim survives strongly. Mean recall is only 3.83% at +5 bps and 41.0% at +8 bps. A zero-survivor campaign therefore has weak exclusion power against modest sparse effects in this tested representation.

## Attack 4 — Statistical Target Matches Economic Claim

Destroyer: the sign-test/hit-rate null should represent positive expectancy generally.

Result: attack succeeds against broad semantic authority. A positive-mean payoff can have hit rate below 50%; the current sign test targets `P(return > 0) > 0.5`, not `E[return] > 0`. The existing diagnostic mean-targeting bootstrap still yields zero BH survivors on the consumed PR #806 corpus, so this semantic defect does not rescue existing near misses. It does narrow what the certifier is allowed to claim.

## Attack 5 — Multiplicity Interpretation

The 648-test global BH burden is real and reproduced. It protects against broad data mining but materially reduces sparse-edge power. Calibration has not yet established that this exact multiplicity structure is optimal for every future MROS hypothesis architecture. Therefore authority is specific to the frozen 648-test campaign design.

## Attack 6 — Representation Authority

The frozen campaign represents the market through a coverage-selected constituent proxy, equal-weight information representation, state/motif abstractions, and fixed 15/30/60-minute horizons. Existing calibration plants effects inside that representation. It does not test whether the representation itself destroys information before certification.

Result: broad calibration authority is blocked. Raw source data are required for a genuine alternative-representation sensitivity experiment; the canonical V3 artifact contains development outcomes and certification outputs, not the source constituent/index parquet.

## Attack 7 — Sealed Tail Contamination

Direct artifact inspection verifies Stage-9 tested IDs and results are empty and `unopened_sessions_scored` is false. No evidence of sealed-tail scoring was found in the canonical V3 artifact.

## Attack Verdict

The provisional statement `PR806_CERTIFIER_FUNCTIONAL_BUT_SPARSE_MODEST_EDGE_DETECTION_UNDERPOWERED` survives independent reproduction for the tested representation and null construction.

A broader statement such as `PR806_CERTIFIER_SCIENTIFICALLY_CALIBRATED_FOR_GENERAL_EDGE_ABSENCE` is rejected.
