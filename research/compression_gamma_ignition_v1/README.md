# Compression Gamma Ignition V1

Research-only discovery of a different NIFTY buy-option mechanism using the preserved historical option corpus.

## Hypothesis

A quiet low-range option contract or whole option wing sometimes shows a first participation shock before short convex premium expansion. The mechanism looks for compression first, then volume or OI ignition, breadth repair, mirror-wing non-confirmation, or directional strike-mass shift.

This is intentionally different from:

- late-day CE inventory capitulation rebound;
- inventory absorption after failed extension;
- leading-wing informed-pressure continuation;
- synchronised surface-discount rebound.

## Governance

- 10 preregistered mechanism variants;
- four expanding chronological OOF folds;
- latest 25% holdout stays unopened unless an OOF survivor is frozen;
- exact next-minute same-contract open entry;
- exact five-minute candle-proxy outcome;
- 0.1% base friction and 1.0% stress friction;
- maximum two signals per session with 20-minute cooldown;
- mirror-side and delayed-entry holdout controls;
- no broker calls, paper trading, live trading, or production registration.
