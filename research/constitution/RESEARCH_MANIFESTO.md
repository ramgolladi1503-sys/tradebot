# Research Manifesto

MROS is built to reduce uncertainty, not manufacture confidence.

We prefer a durable null result over a fragile profitable story. We prefer explicit unknowns over hidden assumptions. We prefer reproducible evidence over persuasive summaries. We prefer destroying weak ideas early over protecting sunk work.

Research begins with information and mechanism, not with a named strategy that must be rescued. A profitable backtest is an observation requiring explanation and attack; it is not proof of structural edge.

The platform must remember why knowledge is believed. A future reviewer should be able to start from `CLAIM-XXXX` and traverse the registered datasets, experiments, observations, evidence, calibration, attacks, and decisions that support or weaken it.

AI agents are research workers, not scientific authorities. Their output becomes knowledge only after repository-backed verification.

Scientific integrity outranks speed, elegance, novelty, and completion pressure.
