# Research Principles

1. **Repository over conversation.** Only repository artifacts can carry research authority.
2. **Observation before explanation.** Record what happened before explaining why.
3. **Information before strategy.** Search for stable predictive information before packaging it as a trading rule.
4. **Mechanism before confidence.** Prefer claims with causal or structural explanation over pattern-only narratives.
5. **Calibration before certification.** A certifier must demonstrate known operating characteristics.
6. **Attack before promotion.** Claims become stronger by surviving serious attempts to destroy them.
7. **No anonymous provenance.** Claims, experiments, datasets, evidence, calibration runs, and decisions require IDs.
8. **No silent mutation.** Material changes create new versions or supersession records.
9. **Negative evidence counts.** Nulls, failures, invalidations, and contradictions are preserved.
10. **Leakage is disqualifying until repaired and rerun.** A contaminated result cannot be rescued by interpretation.
11. **Multiplicity is explicit.** Search breadth and repeated testing must be recorded and controlled.
12. **Economic reality is separate from statistical reality.** Predictability without executable economic value is not operational knowledge.
13. **Uncertainty is an output.** Unknowns and confidence bounds are part of the result.
14. **Scope is a scientific control.** Sprint drift changes the experiment and is therefore prohibited without explicit governance.
15. **Reproduction beats rhetoric.** A weaker claim that reproduces is more valuable than a stronger claim that cannot.
