# Burden of Proof

The claimant carries the burden of proof at every lifecycle transition.

## Speculation -> Hypothesis

Requires a falsifiable statement, target variable, horizon, population, expected direction, plausible mechanism or information source, and explicit destroyers.

## Hypothesis -> Supported Hypothesis

Requires registered data and experiments, reproducible procedures, leakage controls, declared search/multiplicity context, uncertainty estimates, and evidence inconsistent with the relevant null.

Support must survive at least one meaningful perturbation such as alternate period, representation, threshold-free test, or holdout evaluation when applicable.

## Supported Hypothesis -> Certified Claim

Requires calibrated certification procedures, independent attack, robustness evidence, known weaknesses, statistical authority, mechanism authority appropriate to the claim, and a recorded certification decision.

A pattern claim may not silently become a mechanism claim. Authority is dimension-specific.

## Certified Claim -> Operational Knowledge

Requires economic certification, implementation equivalence, executable-data authority, transaction-cost and slippage treatment, operational guardrails, review triggers, and a recorded operational promotion decision.

## Automatic Blockers

Promotion is blocked by unresolved leakage, unverifiable provenance, missing IDs, undocumented sample selection, material code/data mismatch, hidden multiplicity, failed reproduction, or evidence that contradicts the claim without adjudication.

## Non-Evidence

The following do not satisfy burden of proof by themselves: agent confidence, visual plausibility, one profitable backtest, high win rate, a low p-value without search context, a strong Sharpe without robustness, parameter tuning success, or runtime output that was not generated under a registered research protocol.
