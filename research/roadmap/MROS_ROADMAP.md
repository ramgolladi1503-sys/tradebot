# MROS Roadmap

## M1 — Research Governance

Goal: establish institutional research rules.

Exit criteria: Research Constitution, Authority Ledger model, Claim Registry, Experiment Registry, Dataset Registry, Evidence Registry, Confidence Passport, Independent Attack Framework, Research Templates.

Current work package: WP-001 / Sprint-001.

## M2 — Certifier Calibration

Goal: quantify whether the certification machinery itself is scientifically trustworthy.

Planned experiments: synthetic edge recovery, null worlds, power analysis, false-positive analysis, false-negative analysis, representation audit, multiplicity audit.

Exit criterion: quantified calibration report with applicable and invalid operating regions.

## M3 — Information Discovery Engine

Goal: determine where predictive information exists before packaging it as a strategy.

Expected outputs: Signal Registry, information ranking, mutual information, transfer entropy, stability analysis, information graph.

## M4 — Mechanism Discovery Engine

Goal: explain why predictive information exists and discriminate plausible structural mechanisms.

Candidate mechanism families include inventory transfer, liquidity, participation, breadth, dealer hedging, and cross-asset effects. These are examples, not certified mechanisms.

## M5 — Scientific Claim Certification

Goal: certify only claims surviving calibrated statistical, economic, mechanism, and adversarial review.

TradeBot may consume only claims reaching the required certified/operational lifecycle state.

## Work Packages

- WP-001 Research Constitution
- WP-002 Research Ledger
- WP-003 Certifier Calibration
- WP-004 Information Discovery Engine
- WP-005 Mechanism Discovery Engine
- WP-006 Hypothesis Factory
- WP-007 Scientific Claim Certification
- WP-008 Economic Certification
- WP-009 Runtime Integration

## No-Drift Rule

Each sprint has one objective and one exit criterion. Out-of-scope discoveries, implementation ideas, and architecture changes are placed in a future work package rather than silently pulled into the active sprint.
