# Joint-Wing Volatility Resolution V1

## Hypothesis

CE and PE can initially expand together during a volatility or uncertainty shock. A tradable directional move may begin when one same-strike wing retains or accelerates while the other wing rolls over. The persistent wing is bought; the opposite wing is retained as a negative control.

This is distinct from cross-strike catch-up, leadership handoff and broad surface-exhaustion reversal.
