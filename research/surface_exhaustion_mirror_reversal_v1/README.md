# Surface Exhaustion Mirror Reversal V1

## Hypothesis

A broad directional move across one option side can become exhausted before the underlying visibly reverses. When breadth, acceleration, participation or acceptance deteriorate while the same-strike opposite option stops decaying and begins to strengthen, the opposite option may reprice over the next ten minutes.

This is not cross-strike catch-up, selective leadership or the earlier late-day CE capitulation rebound.
