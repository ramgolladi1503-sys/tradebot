# Market Research Operating System (MROS)

MROS is the repository-native scientific research platform for quantitative market research. TradeBot is a downstream consumer, not the authority for research truth.

## Current Program State

- Milestone: M1 — Research Governance
- Work Package: WP-001
- Sprint: Sprint-001
- Status: ACTIVE
- Branch authority: `research/mros-governance-sprint-001`

## Sprint-001 Objective

Establish the institutional rules, registries, schemas, templates, confidence passport, and roadmap required for reproducible and attackable research.

## Scope Lock

Allowed work is limited to research governance artifacts under `research/`.

Forbidden in Sprint-001: strategy discovery, PR806 changes, runtime, trade execution, broker code, MEG changes, UI, TrueData integration, ML models, and new indicators.

## Authority Rule

Repository artifacts are authoritative. Conversational statements, agent summaries, and uncommitted claims are not evidence.

## Promotion Rule

`Speculation -> Hypothesis -> Supported Hypothesis -> Certified Claim -> Operational Knowledge`

Each promotion requires new registered evidence. Levels may not be skipped.

## Required Research Report Footer

Every research report must end with: Observed Facts, Inferences, Hypotheses, Assumptions, Destroyers, Unknowns, Next Experiment, Authority Grade.

## Success Metric

MROS optimizes research reliability: reproducibility, calibration, false-positive rate, false-negative rate, evidence completeness, and reduction in uncertainty. Strategy count is not a success metric.
