# Synchronised Surface Discount Rebound V1

Research-only discovery of a higher-occurrence NIFTY buy-option rebound mechanism.

The frozen hypothesis is that a whole CE or PE wing can become temporarily discounted when contracts move down together with low dispersion, negative surface breadth and volume/OI participation. The campaign buys only at the onset of that synchronised discount when causal stabilisation appears through return acceleration, breadth repair, mirror-wing divergence or participation shift.

Governance:

- ten preregistered mechanisms;
- thresholds use prior training sessions only inside four expanding chronological folds;
- latest 25% chronological holdout remains unopened unless an OOF survivor passes;
- maximum two non-overlapping signals per session with a 20-minute cooldown;
- exact next-minute same-contract open entry and exact five-minute candle-proxy outcome;
- 0.1% base friction and 1.0% stress friction;
- mirror-side and five-minute-delay holdout controls;
- no broker calls, paper trading, live trading or production registration.
