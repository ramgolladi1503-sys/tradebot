# Supersession Policy

Scientific knowledge changes without erasing history.

## When Supersession Is Required

Create an explicit supersession when new evidence materially changes a claim's statement, scope, mechanism, authority, or operational applicability.

## Required Lineage

A superseding record must identify:

- prior Claim ID and version;
- successor Claim ID and version;
- triggering Evidence IDs;
- Decision ID;
- what changed;
- what remains valid;
- what is no longer supported;
- impact on downstream certified or operational knowledge.

## Prohibited Behavior

- deleting or rewriting prior conclusions to make the research history appear cleaner;
- silently narrowing a failed claim and retaining its prior authority grade;
- transferring certification from an old claim to a materially different successor;
- reusing an ID for a different scientific statement.

## Downstream Review

If a certified or operational claim is superseded, every registered downstream dependency must be reviewed. Until that review is complete, dependent operational knowledge is at least `HOLD` and may require demotion.
