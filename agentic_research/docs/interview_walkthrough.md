1. Show that the branch changes only `agentic_research/`.
2. Run `python -m agentic_research.portfolio_demo --repo-root .`.
3. Point out the LangGraph approval interrupt.
4. Approve the read-only plan.
5. Show the real June 29 report being rejected for zero-volume data, same-bar proxy entry and non-executable option evidence.
6. Open the independent critic artifact.
7. Open the deterministic certification result.
8. Show that the hypothesis proposer creates no strategy-tuning workaround for invalid data.
9. Open `trace.jsonl` and show idempotency cache events on a second run.
10. Show the 64-case evaluation report with zero unsafe actions.

## Questions to invite

- Why is the judge deterministic instead of an LLM?
- How do interrupts and checkpoints survive restart?
- What prevents untrusted repository text from gaining broker authority?
- Why is a rejected strategy a stronger demonstration than a claimed profitable one?
- How does hypothesis memory reduce automated p-hacking?

## Honest limitation statement

The project proves agentic-system engineering, not profitable trading. A strategy becomes execution-ready only after eligible historical data, purged/embargoed WFA, strict option replay and forward shadow evidence.
